What is Parisien Radio?

Parisien Radio is a non-stop internet radio station for French house, disco house and filter house: sample-heavy loops, sidechained basslines and sun-soaked samples, all day and all night.

It's a live stream, so everyone hears the same track at the same moment. Press play to join whatever is on right now, and pause whenever you like. You'll rejoin the stream live.
